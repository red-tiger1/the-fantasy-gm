ABOUT THE COMPANY

Made for fans who think like GMs.

Fantasy football managers often switch between websites, statistics, and group chats to plan their next move.

The Fantasy GM is our idea for bringing those tools together in one simple platform. Our goal is to help managers stay organized, explore their options, and enjoy the competition.
