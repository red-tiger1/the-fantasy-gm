THE METHOD

Simple Rankings.
Clear Comparisons.

The Fantasy GM uses a ranking-based scale to assign a numerical value to each player. Higher-ranked players receive higher values.

Add the values of the players on each side to compare the total. A higher total means a higher combined value on this scale, not necessarily a guaranteed better trade.
