BEFORE YOU HIT ACCEPT

A good trade starts with a closer look.

A familiar name can make an offer look exciting, but a trade should do more than impress your league. It should help your team where you actually need it.

01

See the whole deal.

Put every player on the table. Compare what each team receives instead of focusing on the biggest name in the offer.

02

Check the GM Scores.

Review individual ratings and combined totals. A clear comparison helps you see whether one side leads or the trade is close.

03

Know your roster.

Think about your starting lineup, bench depth, and weak positions. The right trade should make sense for your team.
