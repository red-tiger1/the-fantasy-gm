Our GM Score

GM Score is our player rating. Higher-ranked players receive higher scores, giving you a consistent way to compare both sides of a trade.

The score is a starting point. Your lineup, positional depth, and league format still matter.

Roster decisions

Think about what your team needs. If you have several strong receivers but need a running back, the right trade may depend on positions as much as the score.

How to read a trade

  1. Review the players each team receives.
  2. Compare their GM Scores and each side's total.
  3. Check the difference, then consider your roster needs.
