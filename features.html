YOUR FANTASY TOOLKIT

More thought behind every trade.

You do not need a spreadsheet full of numbers to start understanding a trade. The Fantasy GM brings the comparison into one place so you can spend less time sorting through an offer and more time deciding what your team needs.

What goes into the comparison?

Both sides, side by side

A trade involves more than the player you are excited to receive. Our comparison shows every player included on each side, making it easier to think about the full exchange.

Individual ratings and combined totals give you a starting point for deciding whether the offer deserves another look.

View the sample trade →
A rating called GM Score

GM Score is our ranking-based player rating. Higher-ranked players receive higher scores, allowing you to compare players using a consistent scale.

Each side also has a combined GM Score. Comparing those totals shows which side leads on our scale, but it does not guarantee which team will benefit more from the trade.

A difference you can understand

Instead of leaving you to compare two totals yourself, the sample breakdown shows the gap between them. A small difference suggests a close comparison on this scale.

That is where your football knowledge comes in. Look at the positions involved, your league's scoring settings, and who would actually make your starting lineup.

Room for your own judgment

A team short on running backs may approach a trade differently from a team with three reliable starters at the position. The same offer can make sense for one manager and create problems for another.

Our goal is to help you understand the offer, not make every decision for you. The final call still belongs to the person running the roster.

YOUR NEXT MOVE

Read the trade like a manager.

  1. Start with the players. Review what you give up and what you receive.
  2. Compare the GM Scores. Look at individual ratings, totals, and the gap.
  3. Check your lineup. Decide whether the trade improves the positions where you need help.
