HOW IT WORKS

Three Steps to a Clearer Decision.

STEP 1

Compare the Players

Look at the players each team would receive and check their individual GM Scores.

STEP 2

Check the Totals

Compare the combined GM Score on each side to see which side has the higher total and how close the trade is.

STEP 3

Think About Your Team

Consider which positions you need. A higher GM Score does not guarantee the better trade for your roster.
