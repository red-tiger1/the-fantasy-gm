ABOUT US

Built around the game we love.

We're Aiden Lin and Toni Ladeji, the owners and founders of The Fantasy GM. We share a passion for football, data, and statistics, and we wanted to bring those interests together in something other fantasy managers could use.

Why we created it

Fantasy football gives everyone a chance to run a team. But when a trade offer comes in, it can be hard to tell whether you're improving your roster or just getting a bigger name.

We created The Fantasy GM to make that decision easier to understand. Our idea was simple: put both sides of a trade in one place, give players a consistent GM Score, and help managers see what they're giving up and getting back.

Why the numbers matter

We enjoy looking beyond the final score. A player's targets, touches, consistency, and role can tell a different story from a single highlight. That curiosity is what draws us to sports statistics.

Our current GM Score uses player rankings as a starting point. We want managers to use it alongside their own football knowledge, because no number can tell the whole story of a team.
