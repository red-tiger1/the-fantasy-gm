THE GM TAKE

Close enough to talk it through.

Side A leads by 0.40 GM Score points. That is a small difference, so this deal deserves a closer look at positions and lineup needs before either manager accepts.

+0.40 SIDE A ADVANTAGE

What would this change for your team?

Side A receives a running back and a receiver. Side B receives a receiver and a tight end. Those are different roster needs, even when the totals are similar. Think about who would start, who would move to your bench, and which position would become harder to fill.
